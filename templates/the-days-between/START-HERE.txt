THE DAYS BETWEEN
English / A4 / editable travel magazine

START
1. Install the four font files in Fonts.
2. Open a copy of The-Days-Between-A4-Editable.pptx.
3. Read Editing-Guide-EN.pdf.
4. Replace text and photos; choose one front cover and one back cover.
5. Export and inspect your PDF.

56 editable pages = 52 main pages + 4 alternate covers/backs.
92 native picture placeholders. No photographs supplied.
Canva import has not been tested. No Canva template link is included.
