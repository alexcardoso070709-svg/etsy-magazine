### Please read

This is a digital template. No physical product is shipped. Photographs are not included. Replace the writing prompts, sample names and dates with your own content.

The editable file is a .pptx document. The supplied PDF was exported and checked with LibreOffice. Interactive use in Microsoft PowerPoint and import into Canva have not been tested. This download does not include a Canva template link. Font installation is recommended for consistent results.

The template is RGB A4 without added bleed or crop marks. Check your printer's requirements before commercial printing.
