# The Days Between — Editing Guide

## Make it your own.

### 01 / START WITH THE EDITABLE FILE

Open The-Days-Between-A4-Editable.pptx in PowerPoint. Make a working copy first. The file contains 52 magazine pages followed by four alternative cover pages. All text, colour blocks, original illustrations and 92 picture placeholders are editable.

### 02 / INSTALL THE TWO FONT FAMILIES

Install the four .ttf files in the Fonts folder before opening the presentation: Noto Serif Display Regular and Italic; Open Sans Regular and Bold. Restart your editing application if the fonts do not appear. The font notices are included.

### 03 / ADD YOUR JOURNEY

Replace names, dates, locations and writing prompts. Add your own photos to the picture placeholders. The story is structured around six days, but pages can be duplicated, removed or reordered.

### 04 / CHECK, THEN EXPORT

Choose one cover and one back cover. Remove the unused alternatives from the working copy. Review all pages, update the contents if you changed the order, and export a PDF. The included PDFs show the unfilled template, not a completed travel story.

## Photos & words.

### INSERTING A PHOTO

The coloured photo areas are native picture placeholders. In PowerPoint, use the picture icon inside a selected placeholder when available. Choose your image, then use Picture Format > Crop to adjust the framing. The image will fill the rectangular area.

### IF YOUR EDITOR DOES NOT SHOW AN INSERT ICON

Insert a photo as a normal picture. Crop it to match the rectangle beneath it, align its edges, and delete the unused placeholder. Keep a copy of the original slide. PowerPoint, LibreOffice and Canva can handle placeholders differently.

### REPLACING THE WRITING PROMPTS

Click a text box and replace its contents. Short paragraphs and concise captions suit these layouts. Line breaks are set manually for the supplied copy; adjust them when adding your own text. Use the Selection Pane to find objects named TEXT or PHOTO if an element is difficult to select.

### CHOOSING IMAGES

Use your own images or images with permission for your intended use. For full-page A4 photographs, aim for about 2480 × 3508 pixels at 300 ppi. Keep faces and important details away from the crop edge. The included editable template does not contain stock photographs.

## The visual system.

### COLOUR PALETTE

Paper #F7F2E9 · Ink #182E37 · Deep blue #243F85 · Clay #BC4F35 · Sand #E6D8C3 · Mist #E0E7EF · Secondary text #647278. Use blue for emphasis, clay for section labels, and neutral colours behind photographs.

### TYPE

Noto Serif Display carries the editorial headings. Its italic is used for reflective sentences. Open Sans carries body text and captions. Main headings are usually 30–35 pt; body text is 11 pt; captions are 8.5–10 pt. Preserve the contrast between large headings and small labels.

### SPACING

The page is 210 × 297 mm (A4 portrait). Typical side margins are about 17 mm. Columns share consistent edges. Keep the footer below your main content and allow white space around headings.

### EDITABLE ARTWORK

The geometric architectural compositions are built from individual PowerPoint shapes. They can be recoloured or removed. Select the relevant shapes and group them before moving an entire composition. The artwork is not a flattened photograph.

### VISUAL CONSISTENCY

Use a limited family of photo treatments. Avoid adding multiple new fonts, heavy shadows or unrelated decorative elements. Check longer text at 100% view to confirm that it still fits.

## Export & compatibility.

### PDF EXPORT

Export from your editing application at A4 portrait. Use the full-resolution or print-quality option. Review the exported PDF, including all headings, crops, page numbers and special characters. The supplied preview was exported with LibreOffice; it is not a test of Microsoft PowerPoint rendering.

### PRINTING

This is an RGB A4 document without crop marks or an added bleed area. For home printing, use the printer’s actual-size setting and check its non-printable margins. For commercial printing, ask the printer about bleed, colour conversion and binding before ordering. Do not describe this file as a certified PDF/X print file.

### CANVA IMPORT

A .pptx file can be used as an import starting point in Canva where that feature is available. After import, check every page, font, line break, crop and layer. Native PowerPoint photo placeholders may become ordinary shapes. This package has not been imported or tested in Canva and does not include a Canva template link.

### OTHER EDITORS

LibreOffice was used to verify PDF export. Microsoft PowerPoint and Canva were not available for interactive testing. Keep the supplied reference PDF nearby when editing in another application. Font substitution can change the layout.
