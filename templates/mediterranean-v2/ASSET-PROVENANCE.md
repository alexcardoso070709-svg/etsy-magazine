# Asset provenance — Travel Magazine V2

## Supplied source material

- `Griechenland-Reisemagazin Brooke & Robin.pptx`: six pages, originally 8.5 × 11 inches. Opening layouts, photographs, small decorative assets and some narrative copy are reused and adapted.
- The 56 JPG pages in the earlier `Kopie von Kopie von italy travel magazine (A4).zip`: layout reference for the extended page library. Selected fashion, food and keepsake images from pages 32, 42 and 49 are used as editable PowerPoint picture crops.
- `Kopie von Italy Beispiel.pptx`: upload exceeded the 32-MiB transfer limit and was not opened. No claim is made that its editable object structure was inspected.

On 21 October 2026 the user explicitly confirmed that the photographs and decorations were permitted for resale in an editable template. This is the basis for reuse; the underlying licences were not independently audited. Embedded source material retains its applicable rights and conditions. Rearrangement does not create an independent licence or a legal clearance.

## Newly created material

- Additional page layouts, diary copy, labels and souvenir-ticket graphics were created for this revision.
- Three new AI-generated illustrative images: Mediterranean coastal bay, village lane and outdoor lunch. These are illustrative scenes and must not be presented as verified documentary photographs of the named journey. No exclusivity guarantee is provided.
- Some reference photographs already include borders or rotation baked into their pixels. These cannot be edited as separate vector elements.
- The Italian cutouts are JPG-derived image crops. Small document-level white masks cover old item labels where needed; masks and pictures are grouped.

## Fonts

Italiana, Allura, Anton and Poppins were obtained from the official `google/fonts` repository on GitHub. Each is supplied with its SIL Open Font License text in Fonts. Preserve these notices when redistributing the font files.

## Verification limits

PDF rendering was checked in LibreOffice. Canva import, Microsoft PowerPoint interactive editing, print production, source licence validity and trademark clearance were not independently verified. No design was published to Canva or Etsy.
