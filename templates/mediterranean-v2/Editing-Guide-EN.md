# Travel Magazine V2 — Editing guide

## Make it your own

### The files

Travel-Magazine-V2-A4.pptx is the A4 version (210 × 297 mm). Travel-Magazine-V2-Letter.pptx preserves the original Greek reference format (8.5 × 11 inches). Both contain the same 56 designs. Use the A4 file unless you specifically need US Letter.

### A photo-led magazine

The opening six pages adapt the supplied Greece design. The rest combine daily stories, layered photographs, handwritten headings, fashion flatlays, food, culture, keepsakes, photo galleries and paper souvenirs. Names, dates and narrative text are sample content.

### Choose your covers

Pages 1–3 are front-cover options. Pages 53, 54 and 56 are back-cover options. For a 52-page main sequence, use page 1, pages 4–52, page 55 and page 56. The supplied Main-52p PDF follows that sequence. The visible page numbers refer to the full library; update numbers and contents after final selection.

### Install the fonts

Install Italiana, Allura, Anton and Poppins from Fonts before opening the file. Each font includes its SIL Open Font License. Do not remove the notices if you redistribute the font files.

## Editing photos & text

### Text

Most magazine text is editable in text boxes. Replace the example names, dates and stories. Keep the general length of the original text and check the line breaks. Text baked into a source illustration is part of that image, not a separate text box.

### Photographs

Most new photographs are ordinary editable PowerPoint pictures. Use Change Picture and then Crop to position your own image. Some supplied opening-page elements are grouped or use picture fills; select the inner shape or ungroup a working copy before replacing it.

### Fashion and souvenir cutouts

The Italian reference was available as JPG pages. The selected items are editable picture crops, with small white masks grouped over old item numbers where needed. They are raster images. To replace an item completely, replace or delete its ITEM group and insert your own cutout. The original source file remains unchanged.

### Layers and decoration

Photo mats, captions, tape and stickers may be separate objects. Use the Selection Pane to identify PHOTO, TEXT, ITEM and DECOR objects. Group related objects before moving them together. Keep a duplicate of the original slide while editing.

## Export & compatibility

### Canva

Import the PPTX as a new design in Canva where that feature is available. Check each page, font, crop and group after import. The import has not been tested here and this package does not contain a Canva template link. Some fonts or font-upload features may depend on your Canva account.

### PDF

PDF export was checked using LibreOffice. A4 and US Letter versions were rendered. Microsoft PowerPoint interactive use and a physical print proof were not available. Use the supplied PDF as a visual reference after editing in another application.

### Printing

The files are RGB and have no added bleed or crop marks. Confirm the required colour profile, bleed and binding with your printer. Image detail is limited by the resolution of the supplied assets, especially the JPG cutouts and small route photographs.
