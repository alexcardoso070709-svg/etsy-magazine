## 2. Purpose and categories of information

If the Pinterest account owner authorizes access through Pinterest OAuth, the internal application may access the owner's account and board identifiers, board information, Pin identifiers, published Pin metadata, account-related reporting information, and API operation results.

For content management, we process images, Pin titles, descriptions, image alt text, selected boards, destination URLs, intended publication times, approval statuses, and error logs. Content links generally point to our own Etsy listings. We do not intentionally collect personal data from other Pinterest users through this application.

We use these data to prepare approved Pins, avoid duplicate publishing, publish content at times chosen by the account owner, monitor publishing status, and assess the performance of our own content. Publishing is performed only after the account owner approves each item.

## 3. Legal basis

Where the processing involves personal data, it is carried out to administer our own business content and systems on the basis of **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interests in efficient internal content management and account security), insofar as applicable. Where another legal basis is required for a specific activity, we will establish that basis before undertaking the activity. Pinterest account authorization is obtained through the official OAuth process; it is not a substitute for a legal basis where one is required.

## 4. Service providers and disclosures

We use **Pinterest** to authenticate our business account and create and manage Pins through its API, and **Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script** to store content plans and run approved publishing operations. Information needed to complete these operations is shared with the relevant platform. This policy is publicly hosted on **GitHub**.

The application does not sell personal information, share Pinterest account data with advertising data brokers, or offer third parties access to its API credentials.

Processing by these service providers may involve locations outside the European Economic Area. The applicable platform privacy notices and contractual safeguards govern their processing, where relevant.

## 5. Storage and security

Publishing plans and processing records are stored in restricted Google Workspace files. Access tokens and other authentication credentials are intended to be stored privately in Google Apps Script properties and must not be included in publicly accessible documents or repositories.

Only authorized people managing AC Visuals may access the internal workflow. Pinterest account authorization can be revoked through Pinterest account settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not by itself erase earlier business records.

## 6. Retention

We retain operational data only while needed to operate the workflow, investigate errors, maintain accurate publishing records, and meet legal obligations. Unneeded records may be deleted or anonymized. Applicable retention periods depend on the nature of the record and will be reviewed periodically.
